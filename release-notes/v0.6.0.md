# v0.6.0 — READ A BOOK

The Resonance Archives are a library now, not scenery. Every modeled book in
their floor clutter and book-bearing shelves can be taken as an actual readable
book.

## Highlights

- Vanilla `bookpile`, `bookstack`, and `bookrow` clutter converts into
  persistent piles of distinct readable volumes, including clutter already
  present in saved worlds.
- Intact, ruined, mixed-clutter, and lore bookshelves keep their wooden frames
  while every modeled book becomes removable. Right-click takes one; Ctrl +
  right-click takes several.
- Remaining shelf books keep the exact leaning, stacked, and shelved poses
  authored into each original ruin shape instead of rearranging as books are
  removed.
- Displays are populated mostly with Liber Terra volumes and occasional
  vanilla research or Jonas lore books. Lore shelves still give their specially
  marked discovery book first.
- Removed books persist as real inventory contents, so a partially emptied pile
  or shelf survives saving and reloading.

## Install

1. Download `LiberTerra-0.6.0.zip`.
2. Put the ZIP in your Vintage Story `Mods` folder, or extract it to
   `Mods/LiberTerra`.
3. Enable **Liber Terra** in the mod manager.

Requires Vintage Story **1.22.x**.
